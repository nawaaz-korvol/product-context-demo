# Decision 002 — propose a focused pilot

**Recorded:** 2026-09-28. **Kind:** fictional scope-change proposal.

**Acceptance:** determined by the associated PR, not by this file's existence. If merged, this replaces [Decision 001](001-initial-direction.md)'s broad discovery direction for the next pilot; Decision 001 remains as history.

## Change and rationale

Focus the next pilot on a staff-defined checklist and missing-document visibility. [Source 002](../research/002-workflow-observations.md) introduces a possible visibility problem and a counterexample. A smaller pilot would let the team test that hypothesis before committing to full onboarding.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Full onboarding: spans several unvalidated problems and obscures what produces any benefit.
- Reminders first: may help chasing, but does not directly resolve unclear requirements.
- Checklist first: chosen for the proposed experiment; cannot fix delays caused by unavailable documents.

Defer reminders and automated assessment. Keep acceptability decisions with staff. Validate the problem, baseline, and success criteria through the [discovery task](https://github.com/nawaaz-korvol/product-context-demo/issues/1).

## Review needed

The product lead must decide whether this is a useful next pilot. This example asserts no real approval, research outcome, build commitment, or release.
